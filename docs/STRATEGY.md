# Smart.Supply — Concept & Revenue Strategy

## The winning idea
**Smart.Supply = the AI-powered sourcing, procurement & supply-chain intelligence hub.**
Buyers come for free tools, guides and supplier discovery; they leave an RFQ. Suppliers, 3PLs and software vendors pay to reach them.

### Why this beats the alternatives
| Concept | Ad CPC tier | Lead value | Defensibility | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B2B sourcing + supply-chain hub (chosen)** | Logistics $20–25 median, SaaS/ERP $70–300 | $75–$800 per B2B lead (industry CPL benchmarks) | Tools + directory + UGC RFQs compound | **Winner** |
| Smart-home gadget store ("smart supplies") | $1–3 | Low (affiliate 3–8%) | Amazon owns it | Weak |
| Office/school supply e-commerce | $1–2 | Inventory + thin margins | Commodity | Weak |
| Medical supply directory | High | High | Heavy compliance, YMYL | Risky |

The domain reads literally as the thing: *smart* (AI, data, tools) + *supply* (supply chain, suppliers, procurement). Exact-match semantic fit for the highest-CPC B2B cluster that isn't finance or legal.

## Revenue stack (7 streams)
1. **Lead generation (primary)** — multi-step RFQ ("Get Quotes from Verified Suppliers"), Freight/3PL quote, Software Advisor match. Sell per lead ($25–$250) or per engaged RFQ (Thomasnet model).
2. **Google AdSense** — leaderboard, in-article, sidebar, in-feed slots on content, tools and glossary (tools pages = high dwell time).
3. **YouTube** — video hub embeds own channel + curated education; drives watch-time and channel growth; sponsor integrations.
4. **Sponsorships / advertising** — Charter / Platinum / Gold site sponsors, newsletter slots, sponsored guides, "Top 100" list title sponsor.
5. **Affiliate / directory listings** — software directory "Visit site" links (SaaS affiliate 20–30% recurring), paid Verified Supplier tiers.
6. **Talent & jobs** — paid job posts, featured listings, talent network placement fees.
7. **Donations / supporter memberships** — support ongoing operations, contests and prizes.

## Traffic engines
- Calculators (EOQ, safety stock, reorder point, landed cost, CBM, volumetric weight, carbon, inventory turnover) — evergreen, linkable.
- Glossary (80+ terms) — long-tail SEO.
- Guides targeting "what is / how to" queries — AdSense inventory.
- Contests & community — backlinks and social shares.
