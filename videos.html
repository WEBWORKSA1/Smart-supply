A learning path for buyers and logistics teams

Video is the fastest way to get a new team member productive. We suggest working through the channels above in this order, pairing each step with one of our written guides and calculators so the ideas turn into decisions.

  1. Foundations (week 1). Start with short explainers from AbcSupplyChain and SCMDOJO on the plan–source–make–deliver–return model, then read What is supply chain management?
  2. Inventory math (week 2). Watch Supply Chain Secrets on safety stock and reorder points, then practise with our safety stock and EOQ calculators.
  3. Procurement (week 3). CIPS content on ethics, category management and negotiation pairs well with How to write an RFQ and How to vet a supplier.
  4. Freight and trade (week 4). Follow FreightWaves for market context and read Incoterms 2020 explained and Freight modes compared.
  5. Strategy and analytics (ongoing). MIT CTL lectures and Lokad's quantitative deep dives show where the discipline is heading — forecasting, network design and AI.

Preparing for a certification? ASCM's channel supports the CPIM, CSCP and CLTD pathways, and CIPS covers its procurement qualifications.
