Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. Free to enter. Void where prohibited by law.
  2. Eligibility: individuals 18+ worldwide, except where contests are restricted by local law. Employees of Smart.Supply and prize sponsors are not eligible.
  3. Entry period: opens 18 Sept 2026 and closes 15 Dec 2026, 23:59 UTC.
  4. Judging: a panel scores entries against the published criteria. This is a contest of skill; winners are not selected at random. People's Choice is decided by reader votes, one per person.
  5. Prizes: as listed above, paid via bank transfer or PayPal within 30 days of announcement. Winners are responsible for any taxes. Prizes are non-transferable.
  6. Content: entrants keep ownership but grant Smart.Supply a non-exclusive license to publish and promote entries. Do not include confidential information.
  7. Privacy: entrant data is used only to administer the contest and, if opted in, for newsletters. See the privacy policy.
  8. Canada / Quebec residents: a skill-testing question may be required to claim a prize, in line with applicable law.
