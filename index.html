FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Smart.Supply free for buyers?

Yes. Posting RFQs, using calculators, reading guides and browsing the directory are free for buyers. Suppliers pay for verified listings and lead access.

How are suppliers vetted?

Verified and Premium suppliers submit business registration, certifications and references, which we review before badges are granted. Always run your own due diligence and samples before large orders.

How fast will I get quotes?

Our target is first responses within 48 business hours for complete RFQs in covered categories. Detailed specs, quantities and target prices speed things up.

Will my details be shared?

Only with the matched suppliers (up to 5) relevant to your request, and only with your consent on the RFQ form. We never sell your email to list brokers.

Can I advertise or sponsor a category?

Yes — display ads, newsletter sponsorships, sponsored guides, video integrations and category sponsorships are available. See the Advertise page for the media kit.
