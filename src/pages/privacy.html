Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Who we are

Smart.Supply ("we", "us") operates the website smart.supply. Contact us via the contact page for any privacy request.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you submit: RFQs, supplier applications, job posts, contest entries, newsletter sign-ups and messages (e.g., name, email, company, phone, request details).
  • Automatic data: device, browser, pages visited and referral source (including UTM parameters) via cookies and similar technologies.
  • Local storage: preferences (theme, cookie choice) and form drafts are stored in your browser.

3. How we use it

  • To route your RFQ to matched suppliers only with your consent.
  • To respond to inquiries, run contests and send newsletters you opted into.
  • To measure and improve the site, and to show advertising.

4. Advertising & Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You may opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If you choose "Essential only" in our cookie banner, we request non-personalized ads. See how Google uses data from partner sites.

5. Sharing

We share data with matched suppliers/logistics partners you approve, and with service providers (hosting, form processing, email, analytics, payment processors) under confidentiality obligations. We do not sell personal information.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Canada PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25), you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Retention & security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above and apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

8. Children

The site is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 16.

9. Changes

We will post updates on this page with a revised date.
