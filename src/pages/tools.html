📐 Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)

Balances ordering cost against holding cost to find the cheapest order size.

EOQ = √(2 × D × S ÷ H)

🛡️ Safety Stock

Accounts for both demand and lead-time variability at your target service level.

SS = Z × √(LT × σd² + d² × σLT²)

🔔 Reorder Point

Know exactly when to place the next purchase order.

ROP = daily demand × lead time + safety stock

🧾 Landed Cost

The real cost of an imported unit. Duty here is calculated on CIF value (goods + freight + insurance), as in the EU, UK and India. Countries such as the US and Canada assess duty closer to FOB value — for those, enter freight and insurance under “fees” so they stay out of the duty base.

🚢 CBM & Container Load

Carton volume and how many fit in standard containers (by volume and payload).

CBM = L × W × H (cm) ÷ 1,000,000

✈️ Volumetric & Chargeable Weight

Carriers bill the greater of actual and dimensional weight.

Air: L×W×H ÷ 6000 · Courier: L×W×H ÷ 5000

🔄 Inventory Turnover & Days of Inventory

Turnover = COGS ÷ average inventory · DOI = 365 ÷ turnover

🌱 Shipping CO₂ Estimate

Quick tank-to-wheel style estimate from typical average emission factors by mode.

CO₂e = tonnes × km × factor
