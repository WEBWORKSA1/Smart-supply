Editorial policy & methodology

How we create, update and fund the content on Smart.Supply.

Guides and glossary

Guides are written for working buyers, planners and logistics teams. Formulas are standard textbook methods, every worked example is checked against our calculators, and each guide shows the date it was last updated. When rules change — a new Incoterms edition, tariff changes, new customs valuation rules — we revise the affected pages.

Daily news brief

Every day we review new articles from established trade publications. We select stories that affect cost, lead time or risk for buyers, write a short summary in our own words, add a “why it matters” note where we can say something useful, and link to the original publisher. We never republish full articles. Stories older than about ten days roll off the brief automatically.

Market pulse

Indicators come from public, citable sources: FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), the Federal Reserve Bank of New York and ECB reference rates. Each card shows the date of its latest data point, because sources publish weekly or monthly. We add notes on how each indicator affects sourcing decisions.

Directories

The supplier and software directories describe established companies from public information. Companies can also apply for a supplier listing; we add them only after confirming from independent sources that the company exists and that its website, address and products match, and we label details we could not confirm as supplied by the company. Inclusion is editorial and free. Any paid or featured placement is labelled clearly, and payment never changes how other listings are described or ordered.

Advertising, sponsorship and affiliate links

Smart.Supply is funded by advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsorships, listings, affiliate links and reader support. Advertisers and sponsors have no influence over guide conclusions, calculator formulas, news selection or directory descriptions. Sponsored content is always labelled. Read the disclosure.

Corrections

Spotted an error? Tell us through the contact form (topic: “Correction”). We fix factual errors quickly and note significant corrections on the page.
