How to use these indicators

Fuel first. Diesel feeds truckload and LTL fuel surcharges within one to four weeks, and crude oil drives bunker surcharges on ocean freight. When both rise, budget higher inland and ocean costs on your next shipments.

Pressure and prices. A positive Global Supply Chain Pressure Index means delivery times and backlogs are tighter than normal. Combined with rising ocean freight producer prices, it signals a good moment to lock in contract rates and add buffer stock.

Volumes. The Cass and BTS freight indexes show whether freight demand is growing. Falling volumes usually give shippers more negotiating power with carriers.

Currency. A stronger supplier currency raises your purchase price even when the quote doesn't change. Re-run the landed cost calculator with today's rate before you confirm a purchase order.
